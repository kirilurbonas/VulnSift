# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately at the email address on [@kirilurbonas](https://github.com/kirilurbonas)'s GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove content and restrict or ban participants who break these rules.
